# Join the Fitness Quest RPG test

Thanks for helping test **Fitness Quest RPG** before public launch.

---

## Join internal testing

1. Open this link on an **Android phone** signed into the Google account you want to use for testing:  
   **[Join the internal test](https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701161394189462165)**
2. Tap **Accept** / **Become a tester** (wording may vary).
3. Wait a minute, then tap **Download it on Google Play** (or open the Play Store listing from that page).
4. Install **Fitness Quest RPG** and sign in with Google or email/password.

Direct link:  
https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701161394189462165

---

## What to try

- Create an account and finish onboarding  
- Log a short workout (or freestyle session)  
- Check Hero, Battle, Shop, and Allies  
- Optional: Wear OS companion / Health Connect if you use them

You can leave the tester program anytime from the same Play internal test page, then uninstall the app if you want.
