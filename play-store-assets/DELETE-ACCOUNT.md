# Delete your Fitness Quest RPG account

**App:** Fitness Quest RPG  
**Package:** `com.fitnessquest.rpg`

This page explains how to permanently delete your Fitness Quest RPG account and related data.

---

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open **Fitness Quest RPG** on your phone.
2. Open **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm:
   - **Google accounts:** approve the Google confirmation prompt.
   - **Email/password accounts:** enter your password, then confirm.
5. When finished, the app signs you out to a guest session. You can create a new account later if you want.

You must be signed in to use in-app deletion.

---

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app flow (lost device, cannot sign in, etc.):

1. Email [clefebvre81@gmail.com](mailto:clefebvre81@gmail.com) from the **same email address** used for your Fitness Quest RPG account (Google or email/password sign-in).
2. Use the subject line: **Fitness Quest RPG account deletion request**
3. Include:
   - Your Fitness Quest RPG **username** (if you set one)
   - The **email** on the account
4. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion.

You do **not** need to keep the app installed to make this email request.

---

## What we delete

When your request is processed (in-app or by email), we delete or permanently remove access to:

- Your **Firebase Authentication** account (sign-in)
- Cloud hero backup and workout session history tied to that account
- Your **username** claim (so others can use the name later)
- Your **leaderboard / rivals** entry
- Membership in any **party** or **guild** (you are removed from the roster)
- Local hero progress on the device that completes in-app deletion

## What we may keep temporarily

- Limited records needed for security, abuse prevention, or legal compliance (for a reasonable period)
- Workout **templates** saved only on a device (these are not your cloud account; clear app storage / uninstall to remove local files)

## Timing

- **In-app:** deletion starts immediately after you confirm.
- **Email requests:** we aim to complete deletion within **30 days** of verifying your request.
