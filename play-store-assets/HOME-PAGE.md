# Fitness Quest RPG

**Train for real. Level up your hero.**

Fitness Quest RPG turns real workouts into an adventure. Build your hero, train in the real world, and watch strength, loot, and progress carry into a fantasy RPG—so every set moves both you and your character forward.

---

## What you can do

- Log real workouts and earn XP, loot, and progression
- Pick a class and grow a customizable hero
- Fight monsters and explore a fantasy world map fueled by your training
- Complete daily bounties and keep your streak alive
- Party up, join a guild, and climb the rivals board
- Optional Wear OS companion for heart rate and live session metrics
- Optional Health Connect for height and weight context
