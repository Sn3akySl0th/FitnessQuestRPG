## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information
When you create or sign in to an account, we collect:

- Email address  
- Password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password in the App)  
- Google account identifiers if you sign in with Google  
- Username / hero display name you choose  

### 2.2 Game and fitness activity data
To run the game and sync progress, we may process:

- Hero profile (class, level, stats, avatar customization, inventory, currency, energy)  
- Workout sessions and set logs (exercises, weight, reps, duration, distance, optional heart-rate related fields, and similar training metrics)  
- Daily step counts used for in-game travel / idle progress  
- Training preferences you enter in the App (for example goals, equipment, schedule)  
- Social / multiplayer game data (party, guild, rivals / leaderboard rankings shown in-game)  

### 2.3 Health and body metrics (optional)
If you grant Health Connect permission, we may read:

- Height  
- Weight  
- Related health history needed to read those fields  

Age used for heart-rate zone estimates is entered by you in the App (not read from Health Connect).  
We do **not** write health data back to Health Connect.

### 2.4 Wear OS companion (optional)
If you use the Wear companion during a workout, the watch may collect and send to your phone:

- Heart rate  
- Steps, calories, distance, pace, elevation  
- Optional GPS location **only during workouts**, if you grant location permission on the watch  

GPS coordinates are used for live workout metrics on your devices. They are not stored as a location history on our servers for advertising or tracking.

### 2.5 AI features (optional)
If you use AI features (for example workout generation or photo/import coaching) and provide a Gemini API key (or the App is configured with one):

- Text you submit (workout notes, training profile summaries, session summaries)  
- Optional images you choose to import  

may be sent to Google’s Generative Language API to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive information you do not want processed by that service.

### 2.6 Device and technical data
We may process limited technical information needed for the App to function, such as:

- Online / offline sync status  
- Wear connection status  
- App preferences (units, notifications, sound/haptics)  
- Standard network requests to our cloud providers  

We do **not** use third-party advertising SDKs or Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics in the current App build.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Create and manage your account  
- Provide gameplay, workouts, battles, shop, and social features  
- Sync your progress across devices when you are signed in  
- Show leaderboards / rivals and party or guild activity  
- Personalize training suggestions and optional AI features you choose to use  
- Improve reliability and support (including responding to deletion or support requests)  

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Where data is stored

| Data | Typical storage |
| --- | --- |
| Local game progress, preferences, body metrics | On your device (local database / app preferences) |
| Account credentials | Google Firebase Authentication |
| Signed-in cloud backup (hero, sessions, leaderboard, parties/guilds, username claims) | Google Cloud Firestore |
| Optional AI prompts / images | Sent to Google Generative Language API when you use AI features |

Guest / unsigned-in play may keep more data only on device. Signing in enables cloud backup and multiplayer-related features.

Android backup settings may allow some local App data to be included in your device’s backup, depending on your OS settings.

## 5. Sharing of information

We share data only as needed to operate the App:

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** — authentication and cloud sync  
- **Google Sign-In / Play services** — if you choose Google sign-in; Wear connectivity  
- **Health Connect** — if you grant permission to read height/weight  
- **Google Gemini (Generative Language API)** — only if you use AI features  
- **Other players** — limited public game profile information you participate with (for example username, class, level, XP/streak/battle stats on leaderboards, party/guild roster cards)  

We do not sell personal data to data brokers or advertisers.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and the App.
